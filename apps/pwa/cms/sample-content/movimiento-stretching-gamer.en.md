# Stretching for gamers: 5 minutes between matches

Five minutes between ranked matches can be the difference between a clutch late-game play
and a tilt that costs you the streak. This video walks you through an express routine designed
specifically for what takes the most punishment when you're in a chair for hours.

## Why movement between matches matters

After more than 45 minutes in the same position, the muscles in your neck, trapezoids and
forearms enter isometric fatigue. It doesn't hurt right away, but the first symptom you'll
notice is a slight loss of precision on the mouse or stick. GG before AFK.

The good news: you don't need to leave your chair or your room.

## The routine (5 minutes, no equipment)

### 1. Neck (1 minute)

- Tilt your head toward your right shoulder — 20 seconds.
- Repeat toward the left — 20 seconds.
- Slowly rotate your neck in a front semicircle (never a full rotation backward) — 20 seconds.

### 2. Shoulders and trapezoids (1 minute)

- Shoulder rolls backward × 10.
- Interlace your fingers behind your head and open your elbows — 30 seconds.

### 3. Forearms and wrists (1.5 minutes)

- Extend your arm, palm up. Use the other hand to pull your fingers toward you — 20 seconds each side.
- Repeat palm down — 20 seconds each side.
- Wrist rotation × 10 in each direction.

### 4. Lower back (1.5 minutes)

- Stand up. Feet shoulder-width apart. Lean forward with knees slightly bent — reach the floor
  or as far as you can — 30 seconds.
- Standing, hands on hips. Gentle hip rotation × 10 each direction.

## The pro tip

Set an alarm at 45 minutes into every session. Yes, even if you're in ranked. Your K/D won't
drop from 5 minutes of movement — your precision will improve over time if you make this a habit.

## Key points

- **Ideal frequency:** every 45–60 minutes of play.
- **No need to stand up** for the first three sections.
- If you have persistent pain in your wrists or neck, consult a professional — this does not
  replace medical care.

*Remember: gamers who move, take care of themselves, and connect. GG.*
